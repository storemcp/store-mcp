Tools

Toggle individual tool modules. Disabled modules are not registered, reducing attack surface.

ModuleWhat it doesState
Posts & PagesFREE
Create, edit, publish, delete posts and pages. Draft management, scheduling, revisions, featured images.
  • "Publish a post about our spring sale using the draft from yesterday"
  • "Schedule this page to go live next Monday"
Media libraryFREE
Upload, delete, list and update media files. Replace alt text, captions and attachment metadata.
  • "Add alt text to every product image missing one"
WooCommerce productsPRO
Full CRUD on products, variations, attributes, categories and tags. Stock management and price rules.
  • "Reduce all products in the Winter category by 20% until Sunday"
  • "Create 5 variations for the Blue T-shirt SKU"
WooCommerce ordersPRO
Read, create, update orders (HPOS-compatible). Status changes, order notes, refunds.
  • "Mark order #4821 as shipped and email the customer the tracking number"
Reports & analyticsPRO
Sales reports, top sellers, low-stock and out-of-stock lists, daily time-series ready for charts.
  • "Show me this week's top 10 sellers compared to last week"
SEO (Yoast / Rank Math)PRO
Read and write SEO title, meta description, focus keyword, canonical, robots directives.
  • "Rewrite the meta descriptions of all product pages in the Shoes category"
Menus & widgetsFREE
Create, edit and order nav menus. Manage classic widgets and sidebars.
Plugin & theme managementPRO
List, install, activate, update plugins and themes. Triggers WP-level capability checks per action.
System maintenancePRO
Clear caches, run DB optimization, check for core updates, get a full system status report.
